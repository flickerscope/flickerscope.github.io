The Problem

Many lights we use every day — LED bulbs, tube lights, CFLs — switch on and off up to 100 times every second. It is too fast for our eyes to notice, but our brain still feels it. This hidden flicker is linked to eye strain, tiredness and headaches, especially during long study hours.

What FlickerScope Does

FlickerScope is a light-testing instrument that makes this invisible flicker visible. Place any light above the scope tube and it measures how much that light flickers — and tells you whether it is safe for the eyes.

What It Measures

Flicker Frequency
How many times per second the light blinks (Hz).
Percent Flicker
How deep the light dips during each blink.
Flicker Index
Overall flicker strength — 0 means perfectly steady light.
Frequency Spectrum
An FFT graph plus a live 3D view of the flicker pattern.

Eye Safety Rating

Each light is rated using the international IEEE 1789-2015 guideline for flicker in lighting.
