How it works

LED bulbs don't give perfectly steady light — many blink 100 times every second. It's too fast to see, but the eyes and brain still feel it (eye strain, tiredness, headaches).

FlickerScope: Light → TEMT6000 sensor → Raspberry Pi Pico (2000 readings/sec) → this app, which finds the flicker frequency, % flicker and gives an eye-safety rating (IEEE 1789-2015).

How to use
1. Plug the FlickerScope into the laptop with the USB cable.
2. Click ⚡ Connect FlickerScope, choose the port (usbmodem / USB Serial / COM) and press Connect.
3. Put the light to be tested in front of the scope tube.
4. Scroll down, type the light's name and click Save Reading. Repeat for other lights.
5. Click Download CSV to get all results in Excel.

Result guide: Green = no visible effect · Yellow = low risk · Red = high risk.
