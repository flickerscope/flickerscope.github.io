How to use

1. Plug the FlickerScope into the laptop with the USB cable.
2. Click ⚡ Connect Device, choose the port (usbmodem / USB Serial / COM) and press Connect.
3. Put the light to be tested on top of the scope tube.
4. Type the light's name and click Save Reading. Repeat for other lights.
5. Click Download CSV to get all results in Excel.

Result guide (IEEE 1789-2015): Green = no visible effect · Yellow = low risk · Red = high risk of eye strain / headache.
